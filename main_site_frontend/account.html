1. Keep a copy of your data

Download your website settings, Studio projects and files, and linked Telegram data as JSON. Passwords, mail credentials and private calendar keys are excluded.

Удаление аккаунта

Permanently removes your website account, all Studio projects and assets, and linked Telegram data. Existing website sessions stop working. This cannot be undone.

Messages already sent to Telegram, offline copies on other devices, operational logs and backups are not erased by this action.

Данные Telegram

To delete only Telegram history, settings, subscriptions and mail connections while keeping Studio, use Settings → My data and account in your private bot chat.

Download a fresh export first. Deletion confirmation is available for 15 minutes.
